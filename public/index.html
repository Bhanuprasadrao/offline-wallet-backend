How It Works

1. Top-Up Online

Use our secure payment gateway to add funds to your wallet from your bank account via UPI.

2. Pay Offline

Scan a recipient's QR code and use our secure Bluetooth protocol to transfer tokens instantly, no internet required.

3. Settle Online

When you reconnect to the internet, your transactions are synced. You can then withdraw your balance to your bank account.

Security is Our Priority

Our system is built on a foundation of security. All offline transactions are cryptographically signed and validated against a central ledger upon syncing to prevent fraud and double-spending. Your real money is always held securely with our regulated payment partners, and we never see your sensitive payment credentials.
