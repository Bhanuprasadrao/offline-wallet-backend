Policy for Transactions

Top-Ups: All Top-up transactions are final. Once tokens are successfully added to your wallet, the transaction cannot be cancelled or refunded.

Peer-to-Peer Payments: All offline P2P transactions between users are final and irreversible. Please ensure the recipient's details and the amount are correct before confirming.

Withdrawals: You can withdraw your available token balance to your linked bank account at any time. Once a withdrawal is successfully processed, it cannot be reversed.
