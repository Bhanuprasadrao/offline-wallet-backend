Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy outlines the terms regarding cancellations and refunds for all financial transactions conducted through the Offline P2P Wallet application.

1. Top-Up Transactions (Adding Funds)

When you top up your wallet, you are purchasing digital tokens that represent a stored value. This transaction is processed by a secure, third-party payment gateway.

  • Finality: Once a payment is successfully processed by the gateway and the corresponding tokens are credited to your wallet, the transaction is considered final and complete.
  • No Cancellations or Refunds: We do not offer cancellations or refunds for funds that have been successfully added to your token balance.

2. Offline Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Transactions

All offline P2P transactions involving the transfer of tokens between users are instant and final.

  • Irreversible: Due to the offline nature of the transaction, once you have confirmed a payment with your PIN and the tokens have been transferred to the recipient's device, we have no technical ability to reverse or recall the transaction. Please verify all details before sending.

3. Withdrawal Transactions (Settlement to Bank)

The App allows you to withdraw your available token balance to your linked bank account, converting your digital tokens back into real currency.

  • Finality: Once you confirm a withdrawal request and it is successfully processed by our payment partners, the transaction is final and cannot be reversed. The corresponding tokens will be permanently debited from your wallet balance.
