This policy outlines the terms regarding cancellations and refunds for transactions conducted through the Offline P2P Wallet application.

1. Top-Up Transactions

Final Sale: When you top up your wallet, you are purchasing digital tokens. This transaction is processed by our third-party payment gateway. Once the payment is successful and tokens are credited to your wallet, the sale is considered final and cannot be cancelled.

No Refunds for Top-Ups: We do not offer refunds for funds that have been successfully added to your token balance.

2. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Transactions

Irreversible: All offline P2P transactions of tokens between users are final and irreversible. The system is designed for instant settlement between local wallets. We have no ability to reverse a transaction once you have confirmed it with your PIN. Please ensure all details are correct before sending a payment.

3. Withdrawal Transactions

Functionality: The App allows you to withdraw your available token balance to your linked bank account. This process converts your digital tokens back into real currency.

Finality: Once a withdrawal request is successfully processed by our payment partners and the funds are sent to your bank account, the transaction is final and cannot be reversed. The corresponding tokens will be permanently debited from your wallet.
