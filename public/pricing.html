Wallet Transactions

To cover the costs charged by our payment gateway partners for processing real-money transactions, the following fees apply.

  • Top-Up (Adding Funds): A small convenience fee may be applied to cover payment processing costs. This fee will be clearly displayed before you confirm your transaction.
  • Withdrawal (Settlement to Bank): A standard processing fee is applied to all withdrawals to cover the cost of the bank transfer. The exact fee will be clearly displayed before you confirm your withdrawal.

We are committed to transparency. You will always see any applicable fees before you are charged.
