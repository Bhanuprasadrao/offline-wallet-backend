Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 29 August 2025

1. Introduction

Offline P2P Wallet ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and services. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your full name, email address, and telephone number, that you voluntarily provide to us when you register an account with the App.
  • Financial Data: We do not collect, store, or have access to your sensitive financial details like credit card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs. All online payments (Top-ups and Withdrawals) are processed through our secure, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateway partners (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe). We only receive a confirmation that a transaction has succeeded or failed.
  • Device and Connection Data: We collect your device's Bluetooth name and hardware address. This information is essential for the core functionality of enabling offline, peer-to-peer transactions and is only used for this purpose.
  • Transaction Data: We maintain a secure record of the transactions you make, including the amount, date, time, and the UIDs of the parties involved, for your transaction history and for system reconciliation.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Facilitate and process online and offline transactions.
  • Provide you with your transaction history.
  • Prevent fraudulent activity and enhance the security of our App.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Security of Your Information

We use a combination of administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored on secure servers (e.g., Google Firebase), and all communication between the App and our servers is encrypted. While we have taken all reasonable steps to secure your information, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.
